Privacy information

What If Studio is a private tool operated by the owner of the What If Studio channel. This page describes the intended local production workflow.

Data accessed and purposes

  • Google OAuth authorization tokens let the application act on the authorized channel without storing the owner's Google password.
  • Channel identifiers, video metadata, upload status and scheduled times support channel verification, upload reconciliation and publishing.
  • YouTube Analytics reports support aggregate performance analysis and creative planning. The application does not request viewers' Google account passwords or their individual identities.
  • Scripts, source references, media, review results and cost records support video creation and quality checks.

Storage and sharing

Workflow records and media are stored in the owner's local database and filesystem. Google authorization tokens are encrypted locally; on Windows, the local secret vault uses the owner's Windows account protection. The owner controls local backups. YouTube receives uploaded videos and their metadata. OpenAI receives production prompts, research excerpts, scripts or sampled video frames used for generation and review; ElevenLabs receives narration text. Google OAuth tokens are not supplied to those content providers.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It is not offered as a public multi-user service. Any future change to data uses or recipients requires an updated description and appropriate authorization.

Retention, removal and revocation

Local production records remain until the owner removes or archives them. The owner can disconnect Google access at Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future API access; it does not delete files, uploaded YouTube videos or existing native publication schedules. The owner can separately remove local records and backups, and manage videos or cancel schedules in YouTube Studio.
