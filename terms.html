Use of the application

What If Studio is a private, owner-operated tool for producing and managing content for its own YouTube channel. It is not open for public registrations and offers no paid service to third parties.

The owner authorizes connected accounts, controls spending and publication settings, and is responsible for reviewing content and respecting asset licenses and applicable platform terms. Automated quality checks cannot guarantee scientific accuracy, copyright clearance or channel monetization.

Use of Google and YouTube remains subject to their applicable terms. The owner may revoke Google authorization at any time. Revocation does not cancel previously scheduled videos; those must be managed in YouTube Studio.
